MailCopilot - privacy notice

Please read this notice before installing. The full privacy policy: https://docs.mailcopilot.io/legal/privacy-policy

Besides talking to the mail servers you set up, MailCopilot contacts these on its own:

1. Update check. About once an hour MailCopilot asks our update server whether a new version exists. The server sees your IP address and the type of your operating system and processor. Nothing about your mail, accounts or usage is sent. On a new installation a choice is shown at the first start, before the first check; you can change it at any time in Settings > About. When it is off, MailCopilot starts no new checks on its own; a check or download already under way may still finish.

2. Diagnostic and usage data. Off until you allow it; you are asked on the first start. It never contains the content of your mail.

3. AI assistant. Optional. Requests go only to the provider you set up, with your own key.

4. Sender photos. By default, sender photos are loaded from Gravatar: a hash of the sender's address and your IP address go to Gravatar. You can turn this off in Settings.

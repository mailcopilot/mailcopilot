# Welcome to MailCopilot

MailCopilot is an AI-native desktop email client designed for speed, privacy, and simplicity. It connects to your existing email accounts via standard IMAP/SMTP protocols and keeps your data under your control. With a built-in AI assistant at its core, MailCopilot helps you read, write, and manage email faster than ever.

## Key Features

- **Multiple accounts** -- connect several email accounts and switch between them instantly, or use the unified inbox to see all mail in one place.
- **Fast, native experience** -- built with Electron and React for a responsive desktop interface with light and dark themes.
- **Privacy-first** -- emails are rendered in a secure sandbox with external images blocked by default. Passwords are stored in your system keychain.
- **Conversation threading** -- related messages are automatically grouped into threads for easier reading.
- **AI assistant (optional)** -- a built-in assistant powered by Claude can summarize emails, draft replies, and help you manage your inbox.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** -- Gmail-style and Outlook-style shortcut presets let you navigate and act on messages without touching the mouse.
- **Scheduled sending** -- write a message now and schedule it to be sent later today, tomorrow morning, or at a custom date and time.
- **Offline mode** -- download messages for reading without an internet connection.
- **Drag-and-drop** -- move messages between folders by dragging them in the sidebar.
- **Desktop notifications** -- get notified about new mail even when the window is in the background.
- **6 interface languages** -- English, Russian, French, German, Spanish, and Italian.

## Supported Platforms

MailCopilot is available for **Linux** (AppImage, deb, rpm) and **Windows** (installer). A macOS version is planned for a future release.
